One-time Google setup (about 10 minutes)
  1. Open console.cloud.google.com, make a new project (any name, e.g. Slicewave).
  2. APIs & Services → Library: enable Google Drive API.
  3. Google Auth Platform (OAuth consent screen): Get started, app name Slicewave, your email, audience External. Under Audience → Test users, add your own Google account.
  4. Clients → Create client → Web application. Authorized JavaScript origins: https://satanicgoomba-cloud.github.io (and http://127.0.0.1:8443 for the Mac). Authorized redirect URIs: the same two, each ending in /.
  5. Copy the Client ID into the box above (it isn't a secret; no client secret is needed).

Google will say the app isn't verified: that's expected for your own personal app. Tap Continue.

Controls

Menus (VR): point with the laser from either glove and pull the trigger to select. Use the thumbstick to scroll the song wall.

Play (VR): cubes ride the track toward you. Slice pink cubes with the left saber and blue cubes with the right, swinging the way the arrow points (dots: any way). Bigger, centred swings score more. Keep your sabers off the spiky mines, and duck or lean so your head misses the walls. Cubes follow the song: calmer in intros and slow-downs, busiest in choruses (the arena lights up gold). Five difficulties, Easy to Maniac: harder means more cubes and faster cubes. A/X: tap the beat to line the cubes up exactly. Pause: B/Y, the Meta button, or turn your left wrist toward you and tap the pause button on it with your right hand. Works with controllers or bare hands (hand tracking).

Screen: click menus with the mouse. In a song, move the mouse to swing the saber that matches the next cube. T: tap tempo. [ and ]: nudge the beat. N: next song. Esc: pause. Walls can't hit you on a screen.
