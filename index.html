Controls

Menus (VR): point with the laser from either glove and pull the trigger to select. Use the thumbstick to scroll the song wall.

Fight (VR): punch missiles as they reach the ring around you: pink with the left glove, blue with the right. Fill the meter, then hold both grips and punch for a Super Punch, or squeeze both triggers for Focus. When a drop is coming, a swarm gathers: double punch (both fists forward) on the last bar before the drop to fire the Drop Breaker. A/X: tap the beat to lock the tempo. Stick click: mark "the drop is here" for this song. Pause: B/Y, the Meta button, or turn your left wrist toward you and tap the pause button on it with your right glove. Works with controllers or bare hands (hand tracking); Super Punch and Focus need controllers.

Screen: click menus with the mouse. In a fight, aim with the mouse and left/right click (or D/K) to punch. Space: Super Punch (hold, release) or Drop Breaker. F: Focus. T: tap tempo. M: mark drop. [ and ]: nudge the beat. N: next song. Esc: pause.
